# Four Timelines (Accounts)

## Account Overview

| Account | Screen Position | Based On | Primary Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Private Account** | Left panel, top | Birth location | Profile, wallet, messages, post history |
| **Personal Account** | Left panel, middle | Family + Social circle | PCB, posts, family tree, social circle |
| **Public Account** | Left panel, bottom | Present location | CVB, elections, marketplace, job portal |
| **Global Account** | Left panel, alternate | Earth/Continent/Country | Global stats, global collective board |
