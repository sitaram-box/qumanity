Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join my village council?

Register with your village location, earn Karma Points through verified actions, and stand for election during your zodiac month from the Elections panel on your dashboard.

What are Karma Points?

Karma Points reward verified positive actions — planting trees, teaching, council service, and more. They power the Qoin economy and election candidacy scores.

How do zodiac elections work?

Each month aligns with a zodiac sign. Villages elect Nayak and Nayika council members monthly; higher levels hold annual aggregate elections.

Is my data secure?

Qumanity uses dual-key identity (public + private IDs), encrypted wallets, and open-source code you can audit. See our Security page.

How can I contribute code?

Fork the repository on GitHub, read CONTRIBUTING.md, and open a pull request. We welcome developers, designers, and documentation writers.
