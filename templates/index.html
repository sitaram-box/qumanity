About Qumanity

Qumanity is a public digital governance system designed to give every citizen a voice, every village transparency, and every community the power to participate in decisions that affect their lives.

It is not a private app, not a social media platform, and not a government portal. It is a shared public infrastructure placed in villages and towns to make governance visible, participatory, and accountable.

Why Qumanity Exists

Today, many citizens feel disconnected from governance. Public funds are hard to track. Raising issues is difficult. Decision-making feels distant.

Qumanity exists to remove this gap between people and power.

How It Works

  • A public Qumanity kiosk is placed in every village or locality.
  • Every citizen receives a secure digital identity.
  • Citizens can vote regularly, raise issues, and track public funds.
  • Every action is recorded transparently.
  • No middlemen. No hidden processes.

What Makes It Different

  • Track every rupee of public spending
  • Participate in decision-making regularly, not once in five years
  • Local digital economy support through Qoin
  • Recognition of community service and participation
  • Equal access for people without smartphones or technical skills

For Villages. For Citizens. For Transparency.

Qumanity is designed so that a farmer, a teacher, a worker, or a student can walk to the kiosk, speak their concern, and be heard at the right level of administration.

Built by SITA Foundation

Qumanity is developed by SITA Foundation (Sekyor Intelligence Tantra), a research initiative working on ethical technology, transparent governance, and community-driven systems for the public good.

Qumanity invites every citizen to be a participant, not just a spectator, in the governance of their community.
