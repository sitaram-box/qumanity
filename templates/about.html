Origin Story

2011 — The Seed

A single mind worked in isolation — designing a system that could heal India's broken governance: transparent, karma-based, where every citizen has a voice.

2016 — The Architecture

The four timelines emerged. The dual-key identity. The nested geography. The Qoin economy. Each piece tested against Vedic principle and modern engineering.

2023 — The Code

Flask, SQLite, open source. Built to run on a Raspberry Pi in a village with intermittent electricity.

2026 — The Awakening

Qumanity is ready — not just software, but a companion from birth to death, for every citizen of India and the world.

The Technology

Qumanity is built on Flask (Python), SQLite/PostgreSQL, and vanilla JavaScript. It is designed to be offline-first, running on village kiosks with biometric authentication. The Planetary Ledger Node Network (PLNN) ensures no single corporation owns your data. Your village node stores your village's truth.
